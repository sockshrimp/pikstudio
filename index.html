Just seasonal stems, natural materials and arrangements that look like they grew that way. Good for your table, better for everything else.

The fine print

aka why we do things the long way.

Floristry has a plastic problem. We'd rather not be part of it.

  1. i.

    Zero foam, zero microplastics

    Floral foam breaks down into microplastics that stick around in soil and water basically forever. So we don't use it. Ever.

  2. ii.

    Next to no waste

    We order for your brief, use as much of every stem as we can, and keep the rest out of landfill.

  3. iii.

    Wrapped in better stuff

    Paper, string, cloth and vessels you'll actually want to keep. Nothing you'll feel weird about binning.

  4. iv.

    Seasonal, always

    We work with what's growing right now. It looks better, lasts longer and doesn't need a passport.
